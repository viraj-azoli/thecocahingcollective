Home — today's sessions, this month's numbers, and what's coming up. This is what you land on every time you log in.

Do this first

Until all three are done, your profile stays hidden from clients — we do not show a coach who cannot be booked.

1. Fill in Settings

Photo, bio, specialties, languages, rate per session, and cancellation policy. This is what a client reads before deciding — it is worth more than an hour of your time.

2. Connect Stripe

The button circled above, under Earnings. This is how clients pay you — nothing works without it. Once connected, this same screen shows your revenue and session history.

3. Set your availability

Click a cell to mark it open, then press Save availability (circled above). Clients can only ever book inside these hours, and a slot disappears the moment someone takes it.

 
Everything else in the sidebar
Clients — everyone who has booked with you, with their full session history in one place.
Messages — talk to clients between sessions, without handing out your personal number.
Content — publish audio, articles or programs to the shared library and your profile.
Intake Forms — questions a client answers before a first session: text, multiple choice, or a 1–10 scale.
Packages — multi-session bundles at a set price, for work that's a programme rather than a one-off.
 
What happens when someone books

They pick a time from your published hours, answer your intake questions if you set any, and pay you. The session lands under Sessions → Upcoming, exactly like this, and you both get a confirmation email. On the day, press Join (circled above) — the video call runs in the browser, nothing to install.

Afterwards the session moves to Past, where you write your notes and the client can leave a review.
