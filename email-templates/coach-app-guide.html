Now that you have an account, here is what each part of it does. Nothing here is urgent — but the three marked Do this first are what stand between you and your first booking.

1. Settings — photo, bio, specialties, rate

Until all three are done your profile stays hidden from clients — we do not show a coach who cannot be booked.

Your sidebar, top to bottom
Home
Today’s sessions, what is coming up, and quick actions. Start here each morning — if nothing is booked today, it will say so plainly rather than making you hunt.
Clients
Everyone who has booked with you. Open a client to see their full session history in one place, so you are not reconstructing context from memory before a call.
Sessions
Split into Upcoming and Past. Join a call from here, and write your own private notes afterwards. Clients never see those.
Messages
Conversations with clients between sessions. Keeps your personal inbox and phone number out of it.
Content
Publish audio, articles, live events or longer programs to the shared library. Anything you publish also appears on your profile, so people can get a feel for how you think before they book.
Availability  — do this first
Mark the hours you are willing to work, then Save. Clients can only ever book inside those hours, and a slot disappears the moment someone takes it.
Earnings  — do this first
Connect your Stripe account here — this is how you get paid, and nothing works without it. Afterwards this screen shows your revenue and the last six months of completed sessions.
Intake Forms
Questions a client answers before a first session. Three kinds: free text, multiple choice, or a 1–10 scale. Mark the form required and it becomes part of booking, so you never start a first session cold.
Packages
Multi-session bundles — say six sessions at a set price, valid for a number of days. Useful when the work is a programme rather than a one-off.
Settings  — do this first
Your photo, bio, specialties, languages, rate per session and cancellation policy. This is what clients read when deciding — it is worth more than an hour of your time.
 
What happens when someone books
They pick a time from your published hours, answer your intake questions if you set any, and pay you. The session then appears under Sessions → Upcoming and you both get a confirmation email. On the day, open the session and press Join — the video call runs in the browser, with nothing to install and no link to send.

Afterwards the session moves to Past, where you can write your notes and the client can leave a review.
