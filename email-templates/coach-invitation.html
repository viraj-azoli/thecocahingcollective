The Coaching Collective is a small, invitation-only group of coaches. We built it because good coaching gets lost on marketplaces — buried under algorithms, race-to-the-bottom pricing, and platforms that take a cut of every session.

We wanted the opposite: a place people arrive already trusting, where you keep your rates, your clients, and all of your money.

How you get paid
You keep 100%. We never touch the money.

Clients pay you directly through your own Stripe account. The payment goes from them to you — it never passes through us, and we take no commission and charge no platform fee. You set your rate, and that is what you receive.

What you get
A profile that does the selling
Your bio, approach, specialties, languages and rate — presented properly, so clients arrive already knowing whether you are a fit.
Booking and availability, handled
You publish the hours you work. Clients book and pay in one step. No back-and-forth about times, no chasing invoices.
Sessions run in the browser
Video is built in. Nothing to install, no links to send, no separate subscription.
Intake forms and client notes
Ask what you need before a first session. Keep private notes per client, and see their history in one place.
Packages and your own library
Offer multi-session packages, and publish audio or writing to the shared library so people can find your thinking before they book.
Messaging and earnings
Talk to clients between sessions, and see exactly what you have earned and what is upcoming.
 
Getting set up — about 15 minutes
1
Create your account
Use this email address and choose “I am a coach”. Registration is limited to invited addresses, so another one will not work.
2
Build your profile
Photo, bio, how you work, what you specialise in, and your rate per session.
3
Connect Stripe
Under Earnings. This is how clients pay you. Until it is connected your profile stays hidden, because we will not show a coach who cannot be booked.
4
Set your availability
Mark the hours you are willing to work. Clients only ever see those.
5

We review your profile and mark it verified. You will get an email the moment it is, and from then on people can find and book you.
