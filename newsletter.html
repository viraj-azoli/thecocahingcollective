When You Don’t Know Where to Start
There are seasons when you know something needs to shift, but you’re not quite sure what the next step looks like.
That feeling sits at the heart of If Lost, Start Here:
Wellbeing for the Anxious, Disconnected or Uncertain.
Rather than offering another list of things you should be doing, the book creates a gentler place to begin. Built around ten research-backed Pathways to Wellbeing, it explores everything from creativity and connection to purpose, nature, and play with practical prompts designed to help you discover what wellbeing looks like in your own life.

You don’t need to have the whole path figured out.
Sometimes you simply need somewhere to begin.
Written by Claire Fitzsimmons and Amanda Sheeren, this book is an invitation to explore wellbeing in a way that feels right for you.

Maybe You Don’t Need Another Answer
When something feels uncertain, our instinct is to search for opinions and strategies. But clarity often arrives when we create enough quiet to hear ourselves again.
Instead of asking: “What should I do next?”
Try asking: “What do I already know that I haven’t given myself space to hear?”
Take the question on a walk.
Sit with it over coffee.
Let it stay unanswered for a while.
