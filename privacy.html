Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 15, 2026

At The Coaching Collective Online, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website and platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, participating in onboarding, or interacting with coaches:

  • Account Details: Email address, password, account roles (Seeker or Coach).
  • Profiles: Display name, profile photos, bios, specialization details, onboarding answers, and preferences.
  • Journal Entries & Logs: Private daily check-ins, mood ratings, and intentions which remain confidential and secure.
  • Communication: Chats, session messages, availability grids, and meeting details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve our connection services:

  • To personalize your experience and display relevant coaching connections.
  • To enable video calling, calendar sharing, and message exchanges on the platform.
  • To monitor platform activities, verify whitelisted coach profiles, and maintain compliance.
  • To send account confirmation, system notifications, or welcome updates.

3. Security & Confidentiality

Your session details, logs, journals, and dashboard information are encrypted and hosted securely using Supabase. We do not inspect your private journal notes or share your communications with external advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted providers to facilitate core app features (e.g., Supabase for database management, Daily.co for integrated WebRTC video rooms, and PostHog for site performance tracking). These platforms process data in compliance with standard security frameworks.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We store your account and journal information for as long as your profile remains active. You can request deletion of your account and purge all related databases by contacting us at our administrator address.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at: info@thecoachingcollectiveonline.com.
